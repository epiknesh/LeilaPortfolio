Leila is finishing her Bachelor of Science in Architecture at the University of the Philippines Diliman, Cum Laude, Class of 2026. That training shows up in her design work: presentation boards taught her how to hold a lot of information on one page without losing the hierarchy, and studio critique taught her to defend a composition, not just make one.

Outside coursework, she's designed logos, visual identities, and merchandise for small businesses and personal clients since 2022, led publicity and visual communications for UAPSA-UPD's Academics Committee, and contributed advocacy design work for Gabriela Youth–UPD. She also builds 3D models and interior visualizations for commercial and residential spaces, work that most graphic designers never get asked to do.
