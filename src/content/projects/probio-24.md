Brand identity and packaging design developed for PROBIO 24, a daily probiotic system structured around two complementary formulations: AM / START and PM / REST. The project spans logo and wordmark design, typography, visual systems, packaging, and digital and print collateral, establishing a cohesive identity that differentiates the morning and evening formulations while maintaining a unified brand system.
