Brand identity rebrand and packaging design developed for Watercolor Hydration, a hydration and skincare supplement brand featuring watermelon and blueberry formulations. The project spans logo and wordmark development, typography, visual systems, product label and packaging design, and social media collateral. The refreshed identity combines fluid graphic forms, vibrant color, and clean typography to create a cohesive, youthful visual direction that communicates the brand’s hydration and skincare positioning.
